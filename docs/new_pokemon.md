# The 66 new Pokémon

GEN 1 and 2's regional forms and the later evolutions and babies of their lines,
after CELEBI (species indexes 252-317: see `FIRST_NEW_POKEMON`). Their Pokédex
number is the national one (a regional form's is its species').

They evolve as this game's Pokémon do: by level, or by an item used like a stone
(trade evolutions take the LINKING CORD; a type's held item works for a form of
that type).

Their pics, shiny palettes, stats and Pokédex entries come from
[pokecrystal16](https://github.com/DanielOlivaw/pokecrystal16) (DanielOlivaw); their
level-up moves, EGG moves and TM/HM compatibility from [PokéAPI](https://pokeapi.co)
(every game's, the moves this game has). Their pics aren't animated.

## Where to find them

In the wild (grass and caves by time of day, surfing, fishing, HEADBUTT trees and
ROCK SMASH rocks), as bosses, or by evolving the Pokémon before them. Trainers use
them too: GYM LEADERS and the ELITE FOUR have one each, and on EXTREME they join
the teams of the trainers they suit.
